# Predicting Global Electric Vehicle Sales

This project analyzes global electric vehicle (EV) adoption using machine learning and statistical modeling methods. The goal is to explore historical EV sales trends and build predictive models for future EV market growth.

---

# Project Overview

Electric vehicles have experienced rapid growth in recent years due to technological advances, environmental policies, and changes in consumer behavior. Understanding how EV adoption evolves over time can help researchers and policymakers forecast future transportation trends.

In this project, we analyze historical EV data and apply machine learning techniques to study EV market growth.

The project will implement two main modeling approaches:

1. **Time Series Modeling (Python)**  
   Used to analyze and forecast global EV sales trends.

2. **Bayesian Regression (R)**  
   Used to study the relationship between EV sales and other variables such as EV stock and market share.
